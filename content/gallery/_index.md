## Coming soon

*Through my lens.*

Here I will share a small collection of photographs I have taken during my travels and everyday life — little glimpses of the world as I happened to see it through my lens. Most were taken simply with whatever camera or phone I had with me, so the resolution may not always be perfect. They are best enjoyed for the moments they capture rather than for their pixels.

I would like this gallery to be a small, quiet corner of the website — a place to pause for a while and look at places, colours, landscapes, streets, and ordinary moments that happened to catch my eye. I will keep adding to it from time to time as I travel and as life goes on.

Unless otherwise noted, the photographs here are free to download and use.
